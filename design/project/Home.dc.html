Three ways in, one way of working.

Whether you are buying, selling or investing, REWAP starts with the place, then the property, then the paperwork, explained by a brokerage founded by a real estate lawyer.

Buying Read the street before you make the offer. Area Sheets, a step-by-step guide to Massachusetts offers, inspections and closings, and searches saved by place. Selling Price the place, not only the square footage. Comparable sales within the same building type, documents gathered before listing, and a listing page that sells the neighborhood too. Investing Know the block before the numbers. Two-to-four family and mixed-use stock across Massachusetts, from the mill cities to the suburbs, with links to the official permit and zoning records behind each block.

A gazetteer of Massachusetts.
