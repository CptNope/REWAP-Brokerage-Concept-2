Overview

Shrewsbury sits directly east of Worcester, separated from the city by Lake Quinsigamond. Route 9 crosses the lake and runs through the middle of town; Route 20 follows the south and I-290 cuts across the north.

For buyers, the town reads in layers: lakeside streets on the western edge, the town center and common, and residential neighborhoods built across several decades beyond them. This Sheet explains those layers, how people get around, and where the everyday landmarks are.

Housing character

The town’s housing is mostly detached and wood-frame, with condominium and townhouse communities along the main roads. Knowing the type tells you what to inspect.

Capes Steep roofs and dormers. Ask about insulation in the knee walls and the age of the roof.
Colonials Two full stories, common in newer subdivisions. Compare lot sizes and the age of mechanicals.
Ranches Single-level homes, many mid-century. Check original electrical and heating systems.
Condominiums Townhouse and garden-style communities. Read the master deed, budget and reserve study.

From the lawyer’s desk. Before an offer on any older or lakeside property, confirm whether it is on town sewer or a private septic system, and check for recorded easements on the deed and plot plan at the Worcester District Registry of Deeds.

Landmarks and parks

Lake QuinsigamondThe long lake on the Worcester line, with the Route 9 crossing and the shoreline neighborhoods that face it.
Dean ParkThe town’s main park near the center.
Artemas Ward HouseThe homestead of the Revolutionary War general, a museum property in town.
White CitySite of the lakeside amusement park of the early twentieth century, now a shopping plaza on Route 9.

Commercial districts

Most everyday shopping runs along Route 9, from the lake crossing eastward, with a second corridor on Route 20 to the south. The town center around the common holds civic buildings and smaller businesses. A full district guide, with recent and proposed development drawn from public planning records, is in preparation.
