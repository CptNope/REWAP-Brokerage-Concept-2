C2 · PhotographyA commissioned shot list

Photograph the fabric of the place.

Real Massachusetts, every region of it, shot in natural light, with materials and streets as the subject. Every frame should be recognizably here and captioned with its place. Placeholders below are art-directed slots for a commissioned shoot, not stock.

Shot 01 · Hero A street of three-deckers on a hill, raking winter morning light, porches stacked, utility lines left in.
Shot 02 · Material Mill brick, close and flat-on, in a river city: Lowell, Holyoke or Worcester.
Shot 03 · Material Painted clapboard and a porch column, shallow focus.
Shot 04 · Land Stone wall and open field in a hill town.
Shot 05 · Water The coast at dusk: a South Shore harbor or a Cape marsh.

Shoot

  • Streets, facades, materials, landmarks, terrain and seasons
  • Overcast and low winter light; honest color, slightly cool
  • Wide establishing frames paired with tight material details
  • People only as they naturally appear in a place, never posed to represent a buyer
  • Caption every image with its place and, where useful, its coordinates

Never

  • Stock families, handshakes, keys, “sold” signs, champagne
  • Drone shots of unidentifiable suburbs
  • Heavy filters, HDR or sepia nostalgia
  • Images that could be any city
  • Photos that suggest who should live somewhere
C3 · IllustrationParcels, hatches, contours

Draw like a surveyor, not a cartoonist.

Illustration is linework built from real geometry: lot lines, building footprints, contours and routes, filled with material colors and hatches. No houses with smiling roofs, no clip art, no isometric towns.

Patterns double as accessibility: material is shown by hatch as well as color, so the map still reads for people with color vision deficiencies and in print.

C4 · DataReadings, always sourced

A number without a source is decoration.

Market data appears as Readings: compact, dated panels with the source, period and sample size printed alongside. One series is emphasized in Navy; context recedes to Stone; Gold marks “now.”

Navy for the series that answers the question.
Stone tint for context and comparison.
Gold marks the current period or the selected place, once.
Direct labels instead of legends. Values in mono, tabular.
No feed yet? Show the Reading in its empty state, never invented numbers.
C5 · MappingA branded basemap, provider-agnostic

The map is the brand’s main surface.

REWAP’s map is styled from scratch, never an embedded default. It is specified as layers and states, so the same look can be rendered by MapLibre with vector tiles today and swapped to another provider later.

C7 · MotionGeography in motion, briefly

Motion explains where you are.

Movement is reserved for spatial meaning: a route drawing itself, a region lighting up, coordinates settling as the map moves, an image revealed along a parcel edge. Nothing bounces. Everything respects reduced-motion settings.
