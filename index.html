Ground rules

What this concept never does

These rules apply to every page here and to anything built from it.

01Places, not people

Content describes streets, buildings and services, never residents. No “safe,” “family-friendly” or “perfect for.” A fair housing rule, not a style choice.

02No invented proof

No awards, rankings, reviews, sales volume, team rosters or history that cannot be documented.

03Every number has a source

Readings print their source, period and sample size. Without a feed they show an empty state, never estimates.

04Demo data is labeled

Listings, prices and comparables on these pages are samples until IDX is connected.

How this demo is built

The original design files, rendered live

Every page here is drawn at load time from the Claude Design artboards in design/project/. Nothing was exported or flattened, so changing an artboard changes the page.

The README in the repository carries the full brand book in text: strategy, voice, palette, type, system, components and the platform model.
