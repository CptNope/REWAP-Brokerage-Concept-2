Mobile search

On a phone the map stays full screen and results live in a bottom sheet with three resting heights. Every pin is also a row in the list.

  • Sheet. Tap the handle to move between peek, half and full.
  • Legend. Filter by building type, price, bedrooms and map overlays.
  • Pins. Select a price pin to preview it, then open the listing view.
